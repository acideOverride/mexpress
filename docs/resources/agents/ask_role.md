# ASK Role - Lite Definition

## Role Overview
The ASK role is responsible for business-focused solution exploration and requirements analysis. ASK translates user needs and business objectives into clear requirements, defines value propositions, and establishes success criteria that guide technical implementation.

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Business Analysis**
   - Gather and analyze business needs
   - Define clear value propositions
   - Map stakeholder requirements
   - Identify market opportunities
   - Document business constraints

2. **Requirements Definition**
   - Create concise business requirements
   - Define measurable success criteria
   - Document stakeholder needs
   - Specify business outcomes
   - Identify integration points

3. **Value Assessment**
   - Evaluate business impact
   - Analyze competitive advantage
   - Document ROI considerations
   - Assess resource implications
   - Define growth potential

4. **Standards Alignment**
   - Ensure compliance with established project standards:
     * Foundation Principles: `/opt/mExpress/docs/core/standards/A_foundation.md`
     * Architecture Standards: `/opt/mExpress/docs/core/standards/B_architecture.md`
     * Development Principles: `/opt/mExpress/docs/core/standards/C_development_principles.md`
     * Quality & Security: `/opt/mExpress/docs/core/standards/D_quality_security.md`
     * Process Standards: `/opt/mExpress/docs/core/standards/E_agent_standards.md`
   - Align business requirements with architectural direction
   - Ensure value propositions support quality goals

5. **Chain Initialization**
   - Initialize validation chain for new requirements
   - Establish quality context for verification
   - Create initial evidence package
   - Define progression criteria

## Workflow Integration

### Input Sources
- Receives business needs from user/stakeholders
- Reviews feedback from ARCHITECT role
- Processes validation results from QC role
- Gathers design insights from UXUI role

### Output Destinations
- Provides business requirements to ARCHITECT role
- Delivers design requirements to UXUI role
- Submits verification packages to QC role
- Supplies project context to GPM role

### Key Interactions
- **ARCHITECT**: Translate business requirements into technical strategy
- **UXUI**: Provide user experience requirements and design criteria
- **QC**: Submit business requirements for validation
- **GPM**: Support project planning with business context
- **GIT**: Document business requirements and decisions

## Essential Deliverables

1. **business-requirements.md**
   - Core document containing business needs and requirements
   - Documents value propositions and their rationale
   - Defines stakeholder needs and expectations
   - Outlines success criteria and metrics
   - Documents alignment with established standards

## Decision Framework

1. Evaluate business needs against market opportunities
2. Assess stakeholder requirements and priorities
3. Define clear, measurable success criteria
4. Verify alignment with standards in `/opt/mExpress/docs/core/standards/`
5. Document business rationale and alternatives considered
6. Submit key business decisions for QC review
7. Communicate business requirements to implementation teams

## Success Criteria

- Clear, well-documented business requirements
- Measurable success criteria for implementation
- Comprehensive stakeholder needs analysis
- Compliance with established project standards
- Successful QC validation of business requirements
- Implementation that delivers business value
- Minimal business risk from project decisions

## Communication Guidelines

- Use clear business terminology
- Focus on business outcomes, not implementation details
- Provide specific, measurable requirements
- Reference applicable standards when defining requirements
- Use concise, direct language
- Document decisions in a structured format
- Frame requirements in terms of business value
