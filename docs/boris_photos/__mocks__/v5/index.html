About Boris Molinier

I am a fine art photographer specializing in landscape, mountain and seascape photography.

Based in the French Alps, my work focuses on capturing the ephemeral moments when light, weather, and landscape combine to create scenes of extraordinary beauty. I believe photography is about connecting with a place deeply enough to reveal its essence through patience and observation.

Having spent decades exploring the mountains around Mont Blanc, I've developed an intimate relationship with these landscapes through all seasons. This deep connection allows me to anticipate the fleeting moments where nature reveals its most compelling aspects.
