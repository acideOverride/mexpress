Alpine Majesty

Witness the dramatic play of light across the Mont Blanc massif in this series capturing the essence of alpine grandeur across seasons, weather, and time.

Photographed over three years from various vantage points around the Alps, this collection represents an intimate portrait of mountains in their most evocative moments. Each image is the result of countless hours waiting for the perfect alignment of light, weather and atmospheric conditions.
