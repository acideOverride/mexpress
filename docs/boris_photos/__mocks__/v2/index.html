About Boris

I am a fine art photographer focused on capturing the subtle interplay between light, form, and emotion.

With over 15 years of experience, my work explores the boundaries between documentary and artistic expression. My photographs have been exhibited in galleries across Europe and North America, and are held in private and public collections worldwide.

I believe in the power of photography to reveal the extraordinary within the ordinary – finding beauty in overlooked moments and spaces that surround us daily. My process involves patient observation and an intuitive approach to composition and light.

Education
