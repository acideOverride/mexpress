## Overview
The MontPC CRM system aims to provide a comprehensive customer relationship and service management platform for computer repair operations, enabling efficient service delivery, customer communication, and business operations management.

## Success Criteria

### Business Metrics
1. **Customer Success**
   - Customer satisfaction: >4.5/5
   - Service completion rate: >95%
   - Return customer rate: >70%
   - Issue resolution time: -30%
   - Customer engagement: +40%

2. **Operational Efficiency**
   - Service booking time: -50%
   - Resource utilization: +30%
   - Processing time: -40%
   - Error rate: <1%
   - Cost reduction: 25%

### System Performance
1. **Technical Metrics**
   - System uptime: 99.9%
   - Response time: <2s
   - Data accuracy: 100%
   - Processing speed: <500ms
   - Error handling: <1min

2. **User Adoption**
   - Portal usage: >80%
   - Mobile adoption: >60%
   - Feature utilization: >70%
   - Self-service rate: >50%
   - User satisfaction: >4/5
