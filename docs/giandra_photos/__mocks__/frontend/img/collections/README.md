# Collection Images for Giandra Photos

This directory contains the photographic collections by Giandra, organized by series:

## Series Structure

1. **RENVERSER LES MONTAGNES** (Moving the Aquatic Mountains)
   - Photos exploring how water simplifies 3D reality into 2D reflections
   - Inverts verticality onto horizontal lake surfaces

2. **COMME A GIVERNY** (Like at Giverny)
   - Inspired by Monet's water lilies
   - Natural water reflections creating impressionist-like images

3. **COSMOGONIE** (Cosmogony)
   - Water as a mirror of the universe
   - Natural patterns on water reflecting cosmic structures

4. **TISSER LA MATIERE** (Weaving the Material)
   - Explores the physical texture of water
   - Ripples, waves, and complex patterns

5. **ESPRITS DE NOTRE ESPRIT** (Spirits of Our Spirit)
   - Evanescent forms emerging naturally on water surfaces
   - Silhouettes and faces appearing in random patterns

6. **Surfing Angels**
   - The only series incorporating human figures
   - Harmony between surfers and waves

7. **LE GRAND BLEU** (The Big Blue)
   - Exploration of the deep ocean
   - Infinite shades of marine blue

## Image Naming

Images are named sequentially within each collection:
- `image_001.jpg`, `image_002.jpg`, etc.

## Metadata

Each collection directory contains a `metadata.json` file with:
- Title
- Description
- Image count
- Original URL
