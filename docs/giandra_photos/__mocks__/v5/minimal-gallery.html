Le Grand Bleu

An exploration of the depths of oceanic blue, this collection captures the mesmerizing patterns and hues found in water. Each piece reveals the fluid nature of reality and the way light dances through liquid matter.

Photographed along the Mediterranean coast, these unretouched images preserve the authentic interaction between light and water, creating abstract forms that challenge our perception.
